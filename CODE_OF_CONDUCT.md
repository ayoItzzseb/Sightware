# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want the Sightware community to be a welcoming, respectful, and collaborative environment for everyone.

Whether you're contributing code, reporting bugs, suggesting features, participating in discussions, or simply using Sightware, everyone deserves to be treated with respect.

We are committed to providing a positive experience for all community members, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or technical ability.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, welcoming, and considerate towards others.
- Providing constructive feedback and accepting criticism gracefully.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and perspectives.
- Helping newcomers feel welcome and supported.
- Keeping discussions relevant, productive, and professional.
- Giving appropriate credit to other people's work and contributions.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or deliberately humiliating others.
- Trolling, excessive hostility, or intentionally disruptive behavior.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Spamming discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- Deliberately derailing conversations or creating unnecessary conflict.
- Any other conduct that would make the community unsafe or unwelcoming.

## Contribution Guidelines

We encourage everyone to contribute to Sightware, whether through code, documentation, bug reports, feature suggestions, or community discussions.

When contributing, please:

1. Keep contributions relevant to the project.
2. Explain the purpose of significant changes.
3. Provide clear descriptions when submitting issues or pull requests.
4. Respect the project's existing architecture and development standards.
5. Avoid submitting malicious, harmful, or intentionally misleading code.
6. Respect software licenses and intellectual property.
7. Be patient when maintainers review or respond to contributions.

All contributions are subject to review by the project maintainers.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for maintaining a respectful and productive environment.

Maintainers may:

- Review, modify, or close discussions that violate this Code of Conduct.
- Request changes to inappropriate or unconstructive contributions.
- Remove content that violates community standards.
- Restrict participation when necessary.
- Temporarily or permanently remove individuals who repeatedly or seriously violate these guidelines.

Enforcement decisions will be made at the discretion of the project maintainers, taking into consideration the severity and circumstances of each incident.

## Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the Sightware project maintainers.

Please avoid reporting sensitive incidents through public GitHub issues or discussions.

Reports should include as much relevant information as possible, including:

- A description of the incident.
- Links to relevant discussions, issues, or pull requests.
- The usernames of the individuals involved.
- Any additional context that may help us understand the situation.

All reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all Sightware community spaces, including:

- GitHub repositories and discussions.
- Issues and pull requests.
- Community communication channels officially associated with Sightware.
- Other spaces where an individual is representing the Sightware project.

## Final Note

Sightware is built around the idea of creating useful software through collaboration, feedback, and continuous improvement.

We want everyone to feel comfortable participating, contributing, and sharing their ideas.

Treat others with respect, help each other improve, and let's build something great together.

Thank you for being part of Sightware.

**The Sightware Team**
